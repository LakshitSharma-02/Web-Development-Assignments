University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

GLA University Greater Noida offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in areas such as Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Business Administration and Electronics.

The admission process generally includes registration, entrance test, result declaration, interview, fee payment and document submission.

Mandatory Requirement: Students must provide valid academic and identification documents during the admission process.

Eligibility

Applicants must satisfy the academic eligibility requirements of the programme they wish to join.

  • Passed the required qualifying examination.
  • Meet the minimum percentage requirement.
  • Have the required subjects for the selected course.
  • Provide valid academic certificates.
  • Meet programme-specific admission conditions.

For B.Tech CSE-AIML, applicants should check the latest eligibility requirements before applying.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official GLA University admission website.
  2. Register online using your personal details.
  3. Pay the required registration fee.
  4. Appear for the GLA Entrance Test (GLAET), if applicable.
  5. Check the entrance test result.
  6. Appear for the interview if shortlisted.
  7. Pay the required token/admission amount.
  8. Upload all required documents.
  9. Complete the admission formalities.

Required Documents

The following documents may be required during admission:

  • 10th Marksheet and Certificate
  • 12th Marksheet and Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate, if applicable
  • Valid Government Identity Proof
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Entrance Examination Scorecard, if applicable
  • Category Certificate, if applicable
  • Other documents requested by the university

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic and other requirements that a student must fulfil to apply for a programme.
Merit List
A list of applicants prepared according to their academic performance or admission criteria.
Counselling
The process in which students receive guidance regarding courses, admission and seat selection.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on academic performance or other criteria.
Registration
The process of officially submitting an application for admission.

Important Terms & Deadline

Students should regularly check the official admission portal for updated dates and announcements.

Important: Always submit the application before the official deadline.
