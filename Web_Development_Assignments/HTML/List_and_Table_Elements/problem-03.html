Academic Rules & Guidelines

Procedure Before Practical Class

  1. Wear the prescribed laboratory uniform and ID card.
  2. Submit the completed lab record book from the previous experiment.
  3. Review the procedure for the experiment assigned for the day.
  4. Obtain permission from the instructor before powering on equipment or workstations.

General Classroom Rules

  • 75% attendance is mandatory for appearing in final term examinations.
  • Mobile phones must be switched off or kept on silent mode during lectures.
  • Maintain strict discipline and decorum inside class and laboratories.
  • Assignment submissions after the deadline will not be accepted under any circumstances.

Academic Terms Glossary

Lecture
A formal teaching session delivered by a faculty member covering theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students gain hands-on experience applying concepts using computer systems or equipment.
Tutorial
An interactive small-group session focused on problem-solving and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Continuous academic evaluation based on mid-term exams, quizzes, assignments, and attendance.
Practical
An examination testing real-world application skills in a laboratory environment.
