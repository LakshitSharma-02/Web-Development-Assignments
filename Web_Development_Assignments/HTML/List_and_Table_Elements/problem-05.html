Glossary & Terms

Open Event
A competition open to participants across all disciplines without specialization constraints.
Team Event
A contest requiring a group of participants working together under a single team registration code.
Individual Event
A solo competition designed to test an individual participant's skill and expertise.
Registration
The mandatory process of submitting participant credentials and fees prior to event entry.
Coordinator
The designated faculty or student lead managing event logistics, schedules, and disputes.
Final Round
The concluding competitive phase reserved for top-performing shortlists from preliminary rounds.
