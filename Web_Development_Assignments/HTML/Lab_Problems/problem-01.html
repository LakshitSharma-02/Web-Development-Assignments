University Overview

GLA University's Greater Noida Campus is designed specifically as an Industry-Integrated Learning Hub to leverage its strategic location in Knowledge Park, Delhi-NCR. Unlike standard theoretical campuses, it operates on a model co-driven by working industry professionals. For more info visit :-- Official Website

Core Strategic Features :--

1.Location Advantage: Located in Knowledge Park, Greater Noida, giving students direct proximity to major IT hubs, corporate headquarters, and industrial centers across Delhi-NCR.

2.Industry Co-Delivered Curriculum:Courses are designed, updated, and partially taught by active industry practitioners, shifting focus from passive memorization to live problem-solving and project-based

3.Early Professional Exposure:Rather than waiting until the final year, students are pushed into live industrial projects, hackathons, and internship frameworks starting from their second year of study.

4.Advanced Tech Integrations:Features dedicated AI labs, cloud computing setups, and specialized centers of excellence created in collaboration with global tech brands like Google.

Campus Facilities

1. Academic & Technical Infrastructure

a. Advanced AI & Cloud Labs:High-tech computing centers integrated with dedicated cloud sandboxes, modern development suites, and AI tools for live project execution.

b. Smart Classrooms:Air-conditioned, multimedia-enabled lecture rooms equipped with digital interactive boards and audiovisual projection setups.

c. Digital Library & Knowledge Center:Extensive collection of engineering, management, and general academic reference books, coupled with subscription access to global digital research journals, IEEE papers, and e-books.

2. Campus Amenities & Student Life

a. High-Speed Wi-Fi:Campus-wide high-speed internet connectivity allowing seamless access to online resources for both students and faculty.

b. Hostel Accommodation:Safe, well-managed residential quarters for outstation students, equipped with 24/7 security, power backup, high-speed Wi-Fi, and study areas.

c. Cafeteria & Mess Services:On-campus food hubs providing hygienic, multi-cuisine meals, quick snacks, and beverages for students and visiting corporate mentors.

Student life

1.Clubs & Student Communities

a. Technical & Coding Clubs :Student-led developer circles focused on web development, AI projects, competitive coding, and participating in hackathons like Devathon.

b. Management & Entrepreneurship Cells:Student groups organizing case study competitions, pitch decks, guest lectures, and corporate interaction forums.

c.Cultural & Performing Arts Clubs: Dynamic student groups dedicated to music sessions, drama, photography, dance, and cultural showcases.

2. Hackathons & Experiential Learning

a. Industry Bootcamps:Continuous workshops on design thinking, modern corporate tools, and AI applications.

b. 24 to 48-Hour Hackathons:Frequent team coding events where students build live prototypes, pitch ideas, and present solutions to corporate mentors.

3. Sports & Recreation

a. Indoor & Outdoor Sports:Facilities for badminton, table tennis, chess, football, basketball, and volleyball.

b. Intra-university leagues and friendly tournaments designed to build team spirit and physical fitness alongside rigorous academics.

Admissions & Eligibility Criteria

1.B.Tech Eligibility:Candidate must have passed Class 12 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, obtaining a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (higher percentage requirements like 65% apply for specialized CSE AI/ML tracks).

2.PG Eligibility:Minimum of 50% to 60% aggregate marks in a relevant bachelor's degree for MBA and MCA.

3.Selection Exams:Admissions are granted primarily based on the GLAET (GLA Entrance Test) or performance in national competitive entrance tests such as JEE Main or CUET
