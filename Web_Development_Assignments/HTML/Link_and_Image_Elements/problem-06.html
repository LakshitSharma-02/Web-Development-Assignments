Comprehensive Introduction

Welcome to TechFest 2026, the premier flagship national technology festival hosted annually by GLA University at its state-of-the-art Greater Noida campus.
Over the last decade, TechFest has established itself as one of Northern India's most prestigious student tech gatherings, drawing over 10,000 aspiring engineers, software developers, researchers, digital designers, and young entrepreneurs from top institutes across the country.
The central theme for TechFest 2026 focuses on Next-Gen Artificial Intelligence, Sustainable Green Tech Solutions, Industrial Robotics, and Autonomous Systems.
This three-day technical extravaganza is designed to foster cross-disciplinary innovation, providing students with a platform to solve real-world industry problems, showcase final-year engineering research, compete against top-tier coding talent, and network directly with industry veterans and startup founders.
Whether you want to build autonomous combat robots, pitch your revolutionary startup idea to venture capitalists, learn advanced cloud security, or experience immersive VR exhibitions, TechFest 2026 at GLA University Greater Noida delivers an unforgettable experience of learning, competition, and technological empowerment.

Event Highlights & Major Competitions

1. GLA National 24-Hour Hackathon: An intense overnight sprint where developer teams build production-ready software and hardware solutions addressing key challenges in Artificial Intelligence, Web3, Smart Agriculture, and Healthcare tech.
2. RoboWars Heavyweight & Lightweight League: Custom-built mechanical robots battle inside a reinforced bulletproof steel arena for ultimate destruction, control, and victory.
3. FPV Drone Racing Championship: High-velocity drone obstacle course racing where national pilots navigate custom FPV drones through illuminated neon hoops at speeds exceeding 80 km/h.
4. GLA Startup Expo & Investor Pitch: Student entrepreneurs receive dedicated booth space to display working prototypes to angel investors, venture capitalists, and industry mentors for equity-free seed grants.
5. Interactive Hands-On Masterclasses: Intensive technical workshops conducted by certified engineers covering Full-Stack Web Development, Ethical Hacking, Kubernetes, Machine Learning, and Game Engine Architecture.
6. E-Sports Arena Championship: A dedicated gaming setup featuring multi-stage tournaments in Valorant, BGMI, EA Sports FIFA, and Tekken with live shoutcasting and cash prizes.
7. Model Rocketry & Paper Plane Challenge: Aerodynamic design challenges evaluating thrust, altitude stability, and payload capacity built by mechanical and aerospace engineering teams.
