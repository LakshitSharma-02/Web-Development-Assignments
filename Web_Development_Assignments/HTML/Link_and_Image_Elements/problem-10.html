About IIT Bombay

The Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, commonly known as IIT Bombay, is one of India's leading institutes for higher education, scientific research and technological innovation. Established in 1958, the institute has developed a strong reputation for academic excellence and meaningful contributions to society.

Located at Powai in Mumbai, IIT Bombay provides an inspiring environment where students, faculty members and researchers work together to solve complex problems. The institute encourages curiosity, creativity and responsible innovation in every field of study.

IIT Bombay offers education in engineering, science, design, management and humanities. Its academic community includes students from across India and several countries around the world.

Vision

IIT Bombay aims to become a globally respected institution that creates knowledge, develops capable professionals and uses education and research to improve the quality of life.

The institute promotes integrity, academic freedom, inclusiveness and respect for the environment.

Academic Programs

IIT Bombay offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs designed to build knowledge, practical ability and independent thinking. Students can select programs according to their interests, career goals and academic preparation.

Areas of Study

Undergraduate students may study aerospace engineering, civil engineering, chemical engineering, computer science and engineering, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, engineering physics, mathematics and other interdisciplinary subjects.

Postgraduate education includes Master of Technology, Master of Design, Master of Science, Master of Management and doctoral programs. Research scholars work on subjects that contribute to science, technology, public policy and sustainable development.

A typical academic year may be represented as Semester1 + Semester2. Students may also calculate academic performance using GPAsemester.

Research and innovation can lead to achievements such as 10th-generation communication systems, renewable-energy solutions and advanced medical technologies.

Campus Facilities

The IIT Bombay campus provides facilities that support academic learning, research, recreation and community life. Laboratories, classrooms, libraries and research centres help students and faculty complete high-quality work.

Learning and Research

Students have access to department laboratories, computing resources, workshops, lecture halls and a central library. These facilities allow learners to combine theoretical study with experiments, design projects and practical problem-solving.

Health and Recreation

The campus includes medical services, sports grounds, fitness areas, student activity rooms and common spaces. These facilities help students maintain a balanced lifestyle while managing demanding academic schedules.

Sustainable campus development focuses on efficient use of energy and water. Responsible resource use is an important part of creating a healthier future for everyone.

Student Life

Student life at IIT Bombay is active, diverse and collaborative. Students participate in technical clubs, cultural groups, sports teams, social initiatives and departmental associations. These activities help students develop confidence, communication skills and leadership experience.

The institute hosts cultural and technical events where students demonstrate projects, perform music and theatre, participate in competitions and exchange ideas with peers. Such events create opportunities to celebrate different interests and backgrounds.

Students are also encouraged to contribute to community development through outreach, volunteering and socially relevant projects. The experience of living and learning together creates friendships that often continue long after graduation.

Respect, cooperation and inclusion are essential parts of a positive campus culture. Every student is expected to follow institute rules and help maintain a safe learning environment.

Admission Information

Admission to IIT Bombay is conducted through officially recognized national-level examinations and institute procedures. Applicants should carefully read the latest eligibility requirements, examination rules, application deadlines and document instructions before applying.

Undergraduate Admission

Undergraduate applicants generally apply through the national engineering admission process. Students should visit the official examination and counselling websites for current information about eligibility, examinations, ranking and seat allocation.

Postgraduate and Doctoral Admission

Postgraduate and doctoral admissions may depend on qualifying examinations, academic performance, research interests, interviews and department-specific requirements. Applicants should review the relevant department announcements before submitting an application.

Important: Admission dates and requirements can change. Applicants should rely only on official institute and examination websites for final information.
