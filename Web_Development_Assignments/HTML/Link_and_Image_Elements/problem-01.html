GLA University

About

GLA University is a premier private educational institution with major campuses in Mathura and Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with a prestigious NAAC A+ grade, the university has established itself as a leading destination for higher education in North India. Founded with a mission to empower youth through innovative learning, research, and technical excellence, GLA University fosters an inclusive environment that prepares students for global professional careers.

With top-tier infrastructure, experienced faculty members, and strong industry partnerships, GLA University continuously modernizes its teaching practices. The institution emphasizes practical exposure, entrepreneurial thinking, and values-driven leadership across all its undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs.

Programs

GLA University offers a comprehensive selection of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degree programs across multiple disciplines including Engineering & Technology, Business Management, Computer Applications, Pharmacy, Applied Sciences, Education, Law, and Humanities. Each program is meticulously structured in collaboration with industry leaders to ensure that the curriculum remains relevant to current market demands and global trends.

The academic framework stresses experiential learning, offering specialization tracks in cutting-edge domains such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Electric Vehicles, and Financial Management. Hands-on training in advanced laboratories and mandatory industry internships ensure students build real-world skills long before graduation.

Campus Life

Life at GLA University is dynamic, inclusive, and culturally vibrant. The campus is equipped with smart air-conditioned classrooms, high-tech specialized laboratories, a centralized multi-story library housing thousands of digital and printed resources, high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity, and comfortable, secure student residences with modern dining halls.

Beyond academics, the university encourages active participation in co-curricular activities. Students can join various technical, literary, environmental, and cultural clubs, or take part in large-scale annual cultural management festivals like 'Maiatree' and national-level hackathons. Excellent sports facilities—including cricket grounds, basketball courts, badminton arenas, and modern gymnasiums—promote physical well-being alongside academic growth.

Admission

Admissions for the upcoming academic session are currently open across all streams. All candidates must qualify through the official GLA University Entrance Test (GLAET) or possess valid scorecards from recognized national-level examinations such as JEE Main, CUET, MAT, CAT, or GATE to secure merit-based seats.

Scholarships are also awarded to high-achieving students based on academic performance, entrance test scores, and national sports accomplishments.

Application Notice: The final deadline to submit completed online application forms and required documents is July 31, 2026.
