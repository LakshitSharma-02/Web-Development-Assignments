Eligibility Requirements

Eligibility varies according to the selected program. Applicants must carefully check the academic requirements before submitting the application.

General Requirements

  • Applicant must have completed the required qualifying examination.
  • Required subjects must be studied according to the selected program.
  • Minimum percentage requirements must be satisfied.
  • Valid identity and academic documents must be available.
  • Applicants must satisfy the university's admission criteria.

For example, students applying for engineering programs may need Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in their qualifying examination. Some programs may also consider entrance examination scores.

Minimum eligibility may be expressed as 45% or higher depending on the program and category.

Application Process

Follow these steps to complete your university admission application successfully.

  1. Create an account on the admission portal.
  2. Enter your personal and academic information.
  3. Select your preferred program and study mode.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Review all information before final submission.
  7. Submit the application and save your application number.
