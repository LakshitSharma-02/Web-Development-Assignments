Welcome to Dr. Shroff's Charity Eye Hospital

Established as one of India's premier non-profit eye care institutions, Dr. Shroff's Charity Eye Hospital combines heritage with advanced technology. Our team provides specialized ophthalmic care across sub-specialties, treating over 500,000+ patients annually and performing life-changing sight-restoration procedures.

Cataract & Refractive Department

Offering micro-incision cataract surgery (MICS) with advanced intraocular lens (IOL) implants, alongside comprehensive vision-correction therapies tailored for patients of all ages.

Vitreoretinal & Macular Services

Specializing in diabetic retinopathy management, retinal detachment repairs, and age-related macular degeneration (AMD) treatments using anti-VEGF therapies.

Pediatric Ophthalmology & Strabismus Wing

Dedicated to early correction of childhood vision disorders, pediatric cataracts, and squint evaluation in a comfortable environment.

Ophthalmic Glossary & Key Definitions

Consultation
A thorough eye examination including vision testing, intraocular pressure check, slit-lamp evaluation, and specialist assessment.
Follow-up
A post-treatment or post-surgical evaluation to assess healing progress, vision recovery, and adjust medication schedules.
OPD (Outpatient Department)
The primary outpatient wing where initial vision diagnostics, prescription updates, and specialist consultations take place.
Appointment
A confirmed booking slot with a specific ophthalmic specialist or diagnostic unit to minimize patient waiting time.

Patient Advisory & Guidelines

Important Notice for Eye Examinations:

  • Dilated Eye Exam: Retinal and glaucoma evaluations usually require pupil dilation, which temporarily blurs vision for 3–4 hours. Please avoid driving after your visit.
  • Goggles/Sunglasses: Bring a pair of dark sunglasses to protect your eyes after dilation.
  • Contact Lenses: Discontinue wearing soft contact lenses at least 3 days prior to refractive or corneal evaluations.
  • Previous Records: Bring your old prescription glasses, eye drop bottles, and previous diagnostic reports.
