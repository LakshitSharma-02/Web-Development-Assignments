Event Overview

About the National Career & Education Fair

The biggest academic and career convergence event of the year.

Welcome to the annual National Career & Education Fair! This mega event brings together over 100+ top-tier universities, multinational corporations, and government vocational institutes under one roof. Our primary objective is to empower youth through strategic guidance, direct networking, and immediate job/scholarship opportunities.

Event temperature inside the air-conditioned convention center is maintained at 22°C. Expected turnout is over 50,000+ visitors over three days!

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. This fair aims to bridge the gap between ambition and opportunity."

— Event Organizing Committee Chairman

Preparation Guide

Pro-Tip for Attendees

Keep digital soft-copies of your documents uploaded on cloud storage for quick QR-code sharing at exhibitor stalls.

Recommended Checklist for Candidates

  • Print at least 15-20 updated copies of your resume.
  • Prepare a concise 30-second Elevator Pitch introducing yourself.
  • Dress in formal business attire to make a positive professional impression.
  • Carry a notebook and pen to jot down contact details and booth notes.

Step-by-Step Attendee Journey

  1. Register online and generate your registration ID card.
  2. Arrive at the venue registration counter for physical badge collection.
  3. Review the organization booth directory map placed at the main foyer.
  4. Attend targeted workshop sessions and submit resumes at relevant stalls.
  5. Follow up via email with recruiters within 48 hours after the fair.

Information, Terminology & FAQs

Key Terminology Glossary

Career Fair
An recruiting event where employers, recruiters, and schools give information to potential employees and students.
Scholarship
Financial support awarded to a student, based on academic achievement or other criteria, for education.
Apprenticeship
A system of training a new generation of practitioners with on-the-job training and accompanying study.
