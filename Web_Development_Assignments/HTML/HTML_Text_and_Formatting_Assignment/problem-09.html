Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. ID Card Policy: Every student must carry their official institutional identity card at all times while present on campus premises.
Failure to present your ID card upon request may result in denial of access to classrooms and administrative services.

2. Campus Property Maintenance: Damaging, defacing, or misusing campus facilities, laboratory apparatus, or furniture is strictly prohibited.
Any structural or equipment damage will result in a fine equal to 100% of the repair or replacement cost.

Attendance

3. Minimum Attendance Requirement: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all theory lectures, practical labs, and tutorial sessions.
Students falling short of the required threshold will face a strict ban from appearing in final semester examinations.

Examination

4. Examination Room Code of Conduct: Bringing unauthorized devices, smartwatches, smartphones, or printed reference cheat sheets into the examination hall is grounds for immediate disqualification.
Violation of this integrity rule will lead to immediate cancellation of the examination paper and academic suspension.

Assignment Submission

5. Submission Deadlines: All weekly lab assignments and course project reports must be submitted through the official student learning portal.
The firm deadline for end-of-term project submissions is Friday, November 20, 2026, at 11:59 PM. Submissions after this date will incur a penalty of 10% mark deduction per late day.

Discipline

6. Code of Academic Integrity and Behavior: Any form of physical misconduct, harassment, or deliberate academic plagiarism in submitted coursework will not be tolerated.
Zero tolerance is maintained for severe behavioral offenses, which carry a penalty of permanent expulsion from the institute.
