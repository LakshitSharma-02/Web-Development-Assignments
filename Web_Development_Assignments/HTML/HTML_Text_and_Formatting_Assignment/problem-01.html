GLA University


About My College

GLA University is a well-known private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. Established in 2010, the university provides a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in fields such as engineering, computer applications, management, pharmacy, education, law, science, and commerce. The university has a large and well-developed campus with modern classrooms, laboratories, libraries, hostels, sports facilities, and various student activity areas.

Overall, GLA University offers a combination of academics ,campus, technical exposure, and placement opportunities, making it a popular choice among students in the region.


Courses Offered

GLA University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs across different fields. Some of the major courses include B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering, Artificial Intelligence &Machine Learning, Information Technology, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and other specializations. The university also offers BCA, BBA, B.Sc,B.Com, B.Pharm, D.Pharm, BA, B.Ed, B.A. LL.B. and LL.B. at the undergraduate level. At the postgraduate level, students can pursue courses such as M.Tech, MCA, MBA, M.Pharm, M.Sc, M.Com, M.A. and LL.M. GLA University also offers Ph.D. programs in various disciplines for students interested in research and higher studies.


Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University is a combination of academics, extracurricular activities, sports, cultural events, and social interaction. The university has a large campus with modern academic buildings, laboratories, libraries, hostels, cafeterias, sports facilities, and recreational spaces. Students can participate in various technical, cultural, literary, and sports clubs, which provide opportunities to explore their interests and develop skills such as teamwork, communication, leadership, and creativity. The university also organizes events, competitions, workshops, seminars, festivals, and other activities throughout the academic year. Hostel life is another important part of the student experience, allowing students to interact with people from different backgrounds and build friendships. Overall, GLA provides an active campus environment where students can balance their studies with fun, learning, personal development, and college experiences.
