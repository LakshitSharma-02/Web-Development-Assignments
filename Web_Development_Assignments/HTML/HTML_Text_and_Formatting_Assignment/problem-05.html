Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the branch of science that studies the composition, structure, properties, and changes of matter.

Common Chemical Formulas

Water: H2O
Carbon Dioxide: CO2
Ozone: O3
Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4
Glucose: C6H12O6

A key concept in chemistry is chemical bonding, which holds atoms together to form compounds.

Physics

Physics is the fundamental science that examines how matter, energy, space, and time interact.

Scientific Units & Expressions

Area: m2
Volume: m3
Mass-Energy Equivalence: E = mc2
Acceleration: 9.8 m/s2

Understanding thermodynamics and kinematics is crucial for describing physical motion and heat transfer.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms and their vital processes.

The primary process plant cells use to create energy is photosynthesis, which produces oxygen and glucose.

Genetic information is stored inside DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid).

Quick Revision

Subscripts : Used for chemical formulas like H2O and CO2.
Superscripts : Used for math and unit powers like m2 and m3.
Mark Tag : Highlights essential scientific terminology like photosynthesis.
