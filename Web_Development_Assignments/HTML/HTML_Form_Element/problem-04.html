Leave Categories Overview

Leave Category Description Standard Allocation
Casual Leave (CL) Short-term urgent or personal matters. 12 Days / Year
Sick Leave (SL) Medical reasons or health recovery (requires doctor's note for > 2 days). 10 Days / Year
Earned Leave (EL) Planned vacations or extended personal leave. 15 Days / Year
Work From Home (WFH) Remote working arrangement approved by department head. As per Project Need
Other Bereavement, jury duty, or unpaid leave. Case-by-Case
